# Our Research

Our research spans three interconnected areas at the intersection of flexible electronics, human-machine interfaces, and health monitoring.

## 1. Human-Machine Interfaces (Haptic Sensing & Feedback)

We design novel tactile sensors capable of perceiving tactile information and the surrounding environment. Sensor signals are then mapped and processed to drive new types of tactile feedback devices that can faithfully reproduce virtual tactile sensations. These systems find broad applications in embodied intelligence, VR/AR, and beyond.

## 2. Health Monitoring

We develop flexible sensors for long-term health monitoring. The sensors capture and analyze a range of biological signals, including electrophysiological signals (ECG, EMG, EEG), biochemical markers (sweat ion concentration, blood glucose), and physiological parameters (respiration rate, blood oxygen saturation).

## 3. On-Device Medical AI

We perform real-time on-device preprocessing, analysis, and compression of the large volumes of data collected by our sensors. This dramatically reduces the amount of data that needs to be transmitted, saving bandwidth and power.
